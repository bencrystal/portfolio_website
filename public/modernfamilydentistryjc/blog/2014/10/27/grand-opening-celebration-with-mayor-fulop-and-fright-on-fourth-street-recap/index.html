October 27, 2014

Grand opening celebration with Mayor Fulop

We had an amazing turnout at our Grand Opening party to celebrate Downtown Jersey City's newest dental office! Mayor Fulop joined us for a Ribbon Cutting Ceremony and took a tour of our brand new facility. He also posted about Modern Family Dentistry on Twitter, where he mentioned the importance of supporting small business and maintaining healthy teeth! As if being a local neighborhood Jersey City dentist wasn't cool enough, we now have the support of our very own mayor!

We were so happy that Loulou Arena from Madame Claude Wines joined us for a few hours to present our wine selection and make delicious wine cocktails. She has amazing taste in wine and we loved the recommendations she made!

Our guests also got to check out our in-office art gallery that currently displays the works of 7 Jersey City artists until January 2015! We plan to continue rotating works from local artists, so please contact us if you would like to participate.

Until next time,

MFD
