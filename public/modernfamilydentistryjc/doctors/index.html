Our dentists for adults and kids

Dr. Jennifer Marin

Cosmetic and general dentist

Dr. Jennifer Marin graduated from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine. She completed her general practice residency at Newark Beth Israel Medical Center, receiving training in treating adults and children with complex medical conditions and special needs.

Dr. Marin is very passionate about facial esthetics. She provides cosmetic dentistry, Invisalign and Botox services to help patients that wish to achieve more facial harmony. She also provides Botox, physical therapy and appliance services to help patients with TMJ and jaw issues related to clenching, grinding and sleep apnea.

Dr. Marin is a confident practitioner with years of experience. She is also devoted to helping patients resolve dental pain and emergencies. We welcome you to a free cosmetic consultation with her!

Dr. Ari Crystal-Ornelas

Cosmetic and implant dentist

Dr. Ari Crystal-Ornelas graduated from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine. Prior to his cosmetic dentistry training, Dr. Crystal was prolific in the art studio, producing many paintings and fine prints. His main artistic focus dealt with patterns of growth and nature. He knew early on that he enjoys working with his hands and that he wanted to dedicate himself to the compassionate care of others.

As a cosmetic and implant dentist, he utilizes these skills to help patients achieve a wide range of complex goals, whether they be small cosmetic touch ups or complex smile rehabilitations.

Dr. Crystal is also devoted to the care of adult and young patients that experience dental phobia. He is licensed in Enteral Sedation by the New Jersey Board of Dentistry after completing extensive training at St. Joseph's Regional Medical Center. He is now an attending professor and lecturer of sedation at the same program. After treating hundreds of children and adults in his career, Dr. Crystal believes that patient comfort and understanding is the key to successful treatment and oral health.

Dr. Rupali Shah

Board-certified orthodontist

Dr. Rupali Shah, a board-certified orthodontist, is committed to providing the highest level of orthodontic care to her patients and truly enjoys working with patients of all ages.

Dr. Shah grew up in East Hartford, CT, and graduated summa cum laude from the University of Connecticut with a Bachelor of Science degree in Molecular Biology. She received her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 2011 from Columbia University College of Dental Medicine and then went on to complete her orthodontic residency in 2013 at the Eastman Institute for Oral Health at the University of Rochester.

Dr. Shah's professional affiliations include: Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics, and member of the American Association of Orthodontists, the Middle Atlantic Society of Orthodontists and the American Dental Association.

Aside from orthodontics, Dr. Shah enjoys dancing, biking, being outdoors during the summer, and traveling. She is also a huge sports fan, so you'll often find her watching the UConn Huskies or the Dallas Cowboys.

Dr. Yasmi Crystal

Board-certified pediatric dentist

Dr. Yasmi Crystal graduated dental school from Rutgers School of Dental Medicine, got her pediatric dental training from Eastman Dental Center, University of Rochester, and has an MSc in Clinical and Translational Research from New York University. Dr. Crystal is a Diplomate (Board Certified Pediatric Dentist) and Examiner of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry.

She is an Associate Professor of Pediatric Dentistry at New York University College of Dentistry, and an instructor of pediatric conscious sedation for the American Society for the Advancement of Anesthesia and Sedation in Dentistry at St. Joseph's Hospital in Paterson, NJ. She is a member of the Editorial Board of the Pediatric Dentistry journal and a member and spokesperson for the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, where she is also a consultant for the Council on Scientific Affairs.

She is a past president of the New Jersey Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, where she has been co-director of continuing education for more than 20 years. In this post, she helps organize and direct continuing education courses for pediatric dentists and their staff, so they can keep current with the latest techniques, procedures and materials.

Dr. Crystal is a leader in inter-professional relations. She is a member of, and has worked as oral health consultant, speaker and preceptor for, the American Academy of Pediatrics. She is currently an oral health consultant for the New Jersey Academy of Pediatrics, and has been a faculty member for the Family Practice MD program at Somerset Medical Center, NJ for over 15 years, with her clinical practice being a rotation site for all family practice MD residents.

Dr. Crystal is available for appointment upon special request only.

A husband and wife team

As a husband and wife team, Dr. Marin and Dr. Crystal provide a welcoming environment for children and adults to experience modern dental care in a relaxed office. As a couple, they enjoy hiking, bike rides, trips to the Jersey Shore and attending live music concerts in NYC. Both doctors are also fluent in Spanish!
