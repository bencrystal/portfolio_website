Services

Modern Family Dentistry offers services for children and adults in a modern, gentle and compassionate environment. Our main goal is to provide long lasting, optimal oral health for every child and adult we care for, while providing a relaxed, gentle and positive atmosphere.

General dentistry

  • High quality hygiene services and maintenance
  • Preventative treatment
  • Procedures to restore oral health to children and adults affected by tooth decay or gum disease
  • Root canal treatment
  • Periodontal treatment: diagnosis, deep cleanings (scaling), Arestin, periodontal maintenance
  • Tooth extractions
  • Oral surgery
  • Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF) for treating hypersensitivity and tooth decay (cavities) in adult and pediatric patients

Cosmetic dentistry

  • Porcelain veneers
  • Same day crowns (with our Cerec system)
  • Tooth whitening
  • Removing or reducing the appearance of tetracycline staining

Facial esthetics and TMJ

  • Neurotoxin treatment (Dysport, Xeomin, Botox) to reduce TMJ issues, migraines, clenching, bruxism and other cosmetic issues
  • Emface: facial muscle toning treatment that produces a non-surgical face and neck lift
  • Sleep apnea appliances (oral appliance therapy)

Implants and dentures

  • Implant dentistry
  • Replacement of multiple failing or missing teeth (implants, bridgework, partial dentures, complete dentures)
  • Rehabilitation of all teeth with All on 4 implants, total tooth replacement with implants, dentures supported by implants
  • Denture repair and adjustments

Orthodontics

  • Braces, retainers and Invisalign
  • Orthodontic emergencies (our general dentists are equipped to handle any issue)

Children's dentistry

  • Pediatric dentist on staff (Dr. Yasmi Crystal, DMD)
  • Laser frenectomy
  • Treatment of dental injuries for adults and children

Comfort and sedation

  • Nitrous oxide analgesia
  • In-office conscious sedation and IV sedation (adult, pediatric and special needs)

Emergencies

  • Emergency dental services and trauma, with same day appointments available
  • 24 hour telephone coverage for patients of record

Technology

We are proud to offer advanced dental equipment in our state-of-the-art office, including low radiation digital X-rays, comfortable and easy X-ray taking, computerized pain-free anesthesia (The Wand), 3D digital tooth scanning (to replace the need for taking molds with impression material) and laser dentistry.

Consultations

We also provide dental consultations for patients that are afraid of dentistry or have complex dental needs that they wish to discuss with a professional.

Questions, or ready to schedule a visit? Same-day emergency appointments are available.
