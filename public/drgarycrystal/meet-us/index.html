Meet The Doctor

Gary S. Crystal, DMD Prosthodontist

Dr. Gary Crystal completed three years of hospital and special training after graduating from UMDNJ in 1993. The experience and commitment to his specialty are visible in the care he gives his patients. Dr. Crystal’s sophisticated level of work in restorative, aesthetic and implant dentistry is the foundation of his practice. He communicates well with other specialists and guides patients through the most complex cases, while delivering treatment in a relaxed atmosphere with the personal attention his patients appreciate. Dr.Crystal is past president of the New Jersey chapter of the American College of Prosthodontists.

Questions, or ready to schedule a visit? Our office is happy to help.
