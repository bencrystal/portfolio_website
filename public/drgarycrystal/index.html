Our approach

Stressing preventative and conservative dental care, the practice goals are to achieve optimal aesthetics, function and comfort for patients in a relaxed environment.

Each patient is evaluated based on individual needs and good communication is established prior to dental treatment. Dr. Crystal can also make subtle improvements to your smile using bonded restorations.

Treatments

  • Dental Implants

    Replacement teeth that look, feel and function like natural teeth.

  • Porcelain Veneers

    Thin ceramic shells that reshape and brighten your smile.

  • Crowns

    Restore the strength and appearance of a damaged tooth.

  • Bridges

    A fixed way to replace one or more missing teeth.

  • Dentures

    Complete and partial dentures made for comfort and fit.

  • Teeth Whitening

    A simple, conservative way to a brighter smile.
